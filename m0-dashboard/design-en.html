Module suggestions and evidence

Module suggestions use the readout temperature and Na⁺+K⁺， without Mg ；Primer prioritizes accessibility of its binding site ，Cas prioritizes accessibility of its activator site ；linker prioritizes the less accessible of the two sites 。 This does not predict amplification efficiency or Cas activity 。

Primer： Preserves the current sequence 3′ end 16–24nt truncations ；spacer： Preserves the current sequence 5′ end 20–24nt truncations ；linker B： composition-preserving permutations 。 Single-module suggestions retain A/C； Joint design may change A first base and C last base to avoid boundary extension 。RCA folding models one reverse-complement unit ； Exact-complement screening checks the junction between two repeat units ， this is not a model of long RCA、 mixed DNA/RNA dimers or RNP model 。

Study oligo IDs 68_RCA_Primer、12_gRNA、6_Padlock_Tm55/45。 Linker boundaries are annotations used in this version ， they may have other functions 。 Changing primer/spacer also changes the matching backbone site ， new matching oligos are required 。

Methods and limitations

Fixed-module mode combines arm Tm with structural accessibility ； Joint mode screens a bounded sequence pool for new primer、 full crRNA and recognition arms 。Tm does not equal the optimal ligation temperature ， a mismatch does not guarantee blocked ligation 。

Tm：Biopython DNA_NN4 / Owczarzy 2008， uses the supplied ions and probe concentration ， low-target approximation 。 total Mg does not equal measured free Mg。

Self-folding ：ViennaRNA DNA Mathews 2004， supplied temperature 、Na⁺+K⁺ uses the configured conditions 、 DNA model flag noGU is False 、 without Mg 。 The drawing is a representative prediction ， not a measured three-dimensional structure 。 Duplex opening is not modeled 、 cooling or ligase kinetics are not modeled ； Not calculated: WT mismatch Tm、 genome-wide specificity 。

Joint design ：primer 19–24 nt，spacer 20–24 nt，GC 35–65%； Retains the current LbaCas12a handle。 new crRNA targets the synthetic RCA ssDNA activator ， not a direct genomic-targeting gRNA。RNA uses Turner2004，DNA uses Mathews2004； no Mg / RNP / genome-wide off-target model 。 Initially generates 16 entries 、 retains 8 for joint screening ， recommends at most 3 sets ， not a global optimum 。primer Tm by 500 nM and monovalent salt ， distinct from the Mg-corrected arm Tm model ； Full rules are included in JSON Export 。

Experimental comparison ： Keep other conditions fixed ， compare Mut、WT and blank 。 When conditions change ， an ID may refer to a different sequence 。
